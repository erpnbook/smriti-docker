## SMRITI Documentation Philosophy
The core design principle is that software should not only process data but also explain its decisions. Every report, score, KPI, alert, recommendation, and prediction within SMRITI must be understandable by business users without requiring technical or mathematical expertise. The centralized Formula Registry and Business Dictionary ensure transparency and auditability across all store operations.
