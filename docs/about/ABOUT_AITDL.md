# About AITDL

## Organization Mission
**AITDL – AI Technology & Development Lab** is an enterprise solutions laboratory focused on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, retail logistics, distribution systems, and business process automation.

AITDL specializes in designing experience layers, data platforms, and intelligence workflows that sit on top of standard transactional engines.

---

## Areas of Research
* **Inventory Forecasting**: Advanced models to calculate weeks of cover and stockout risk dates.
* **Supply Chain Rebalancing**: Logic engines that identify local stock overages and generate transfer recommendations.
* **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Architectures like the SMRITI Formula Registry and Business Dictionary that explain mathematical decisions to non-technical store managers.
