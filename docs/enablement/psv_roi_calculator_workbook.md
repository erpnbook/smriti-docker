## 1. Objective of the Workbook
This workbook serves as a structured, one-page calculator that sales teams and distributors can use to estimate the financial return of implementing SMRITI Party Stock Visibility (PSV). It translates inventory visibility into cash profit.

## 3. The 3 ROI Recovery Formulas

Using your inputs, calculate the following three financial outcomes:

### Step 1: Revenue Recovered (बिक्री की वसूली)
*   **Business Meaning**: Sales recaptured by resolving stockouts through WOC Alerts and stock transfers.
*   **Formula**:
    $$\text{Revenue Recovered} = \text{Annual Network Sales (A)} \times \text{Stockout Rate \% (C)} \times \text{Recovery Efficiency (60\%)}$$
*   **Worked Calculation**:
    $$\text{Revenue Recovered} = 5,00,00,000 \times 0.08 \times 0.60 = \text{\textbf{₹24,00,000}}$$

### Step 2: Capital Released (पूंजी की मुक्ति)
*   **Business Meaning**: Working capital freed from dead stock (reduction of stagnant stock by 40%).
*   **Formula**:
    $$\text{Capital Released} = \text{Active Inventory Value (D)} \times \text{Stagnant Ratio (30\%)} \times \text{Dead Stock Reduction (40\%)}$$
*   **Worked Calculation**:
    $$\text{Capital Released} = 1,00,00,000 \times 0.30 \times 0.40 = \text{\textbf{₹12,00,000}}$$

### Step 3: Margin Improvement (लाभ में वृद्धि)
*   **Business Meaning**: Incremental profit generated from recovered sales.
*   **Formula**:
    $$\text{Margin Improvement} = \text{Revenue Recovered} \times \text{Net Profit Margin \% (B)}$$
*   **Worked Calculation**:
    $$\text{Margin Improvement} = 24,00,000 \times 0.10 = \text{\textbf{₹2,40,000}}$$

## 4. Summary of Financial Impact

Based on the worked calculations above, implementing SMRITI PSV yields:

1.  **Total Cash Profit Increase**: **₹2,40,000** annually.
2.  **Freed Capital Reinvested**: **₹12,00,000** back into fast-moving styles.
3.  **Distributor ROI Shift**: Improves average distributor ROI from **20% to 26%** by turning inventory faster and reducing dead stock holdings.

    > [!NOTE]
    > **Distributor ROI Shift Derivation (CFO Note)**:
    > *   **Formula**: $\text{Distributor ROI \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Invested Capital (Inventory Value)}}\right) \times 100$
    > *   **Before SMRITI**: A distributor holding ₹1,00,00,000 in average inventory generates ₹20,00,000 in annual net profit $\rightarrow \text{ROI} = 20\%$.
    > *   **After SMRITI**: Reinvesting freed capital into high-velocity variant curves reduces dead stock holdings and captures lost sales, boosting annual net profit by ₹6,00,000 to ₹26,00,000 on the same capital base $\rightarrow \text{ROI} = \left(\frac{26,00,000}{1,00,00,000}\right) \times 100 = 26\%$.
