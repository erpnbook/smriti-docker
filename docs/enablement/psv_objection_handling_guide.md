## 1. Handling Brand & Distributor Objections
During presentations, sales teams will encounter operational objections. This guide provides structured, business-oriented responses to resolve concerns without relying on technical jargon.

---

## 2. Objection & Response Matrix

| Objection | Target Audience | Response Theme | SMRITI Standard Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"We already use Excel."** | Distributor / Brand Director | **Visibility vs. Spreadsheets** | "Excel sheets are isolated and lag. They cannot show you when a bestseller runs out in real-time or predict stockouts. SMRITI replaces manual updates with automated reorder planning and network stock transfer recommendations." |
| **"Our distributors won't share data."** | Sales Head / Brand CEO | **Business Collaboration** | "Distributors share sales data because SMRITI provides them with automated reorder calculations that protect them from running dry. It turns their inventory faster, freeing up cash and boosting their ROI from 20% to 26%." |
| **"What if the internet goes down?"** | Store Manager | **Offline Continuity** | "SMRITI POS registers and upload portals run on local browser databases. Transactions are saved locally and synchronize automatically once connection is restored. Operations continue without interruption." |
| **"We have only one store."** | Store Owner | **Scalability** | "Even with one store, SMRITI PSV optimizes your size curves, flags stockout risks, and calculates capital locked in stagnant items, ensuring high inventory turns." |
| **"We already have standard reports."** | Brand Director / Analyst | **Actionable Intelligence** | "Reports show you what happened in the past (historical sales). SMRITI PDT predicts WOC (Weeks of Cover) and flags stockout risk dates 14 days in advance, allowing you to react before sales are lost." |
| **"We have data privacy concerns."** | Distributor | **Controlled Access** | "SMRITI restricts data access through role-based permissions. Distributors only see their own sales and depot quantities. Brand owners see aggregate regional stock without exposing distributor margins to other partners." |
| **"It requires too much effort to maintain."** | Planners | **Automation** | "Imports are automated via standardized Excel/CSV templates with built-in MD5 fingerprint checks to prevent duplicate entries. Reconciliation is simplified with variance alerts." |
| **"We are too small."** | Regional Brand | **Growth Readiness** | "SMRITI provides small, growing brands with the same supply chain discipline used by major enterprise footwear and apparel networks, preventing capital lockup from day one." |
| **"We are too large."** | Enterprise Brand | **Multi-Location Visibility** | "SMRITI's shadow ledger architecture scale-checks bulk transactions smoothly. It aggregates thousands of variant items across hundreds of distributors without slowing down your core ERPNext database." |
| **"What ROI can I expect?"** | CEO / CFO | **ROI Calculator** | "Brand owners typically see bestseller stockouts drop by 60% and a 30% increase in network sales volume by reallocating freed capital into high-velocity variant curves." |
