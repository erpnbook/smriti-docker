## 1. Product Elevator Pitch
SMRITI Party Stock Visibility (PSV) is a real-time **Inventory Visibility Layer** built on top of ERPNext. While traditional ERPs invoice stock and treat it as sold, SMRITI PSV tracks distributor-held inventory, sales velocities, and weeks of cover (WOC). This allows brands to eliminate stockouts on standard sizes, free capital locked in dead stock, and boost distributor ROI by up to 6% without altering general ledger accounting records.

---

## 2. Target Buyer Personas & Main Concerns

### Persona A: Brand CEO / Owner
*   **Main Concern**: Stagnant revenue growth, high dead-stock discount claims, and capital tied up in distributor warehouses.
*   **PSV Value Hook**: Recaptures lost sales by identifying size out-of-stocks early, increasing net margins by 10%–12%.

### Persona B: Head of Sales / Distribution
*   **Main Concern**: Distributors complaining about slow inventory rotation and refusing to take new style shipments.
*   **PSV Value Hook**: Uses WOC Alerts and Network Stock Transfers to shift stock to locations with active demand.

### Persona C: Head of Supply Chain / Planner
*   **Main Concern**: guesswork-based production schedules resulting in variant mismatching.
*   **PSV Value Hook**: Dynamic reorder parameters (ROP) based on actual distributor checkouts.

---

## 3. Key Messaging Points

*   **Visibility Without Ledger Contention**: Tracks distributor stocks through a frontend shadow database, ensuring corporate accounting registers remain completely isolated and secure.
*   **Explainable Analytics**: No black-box AI algorithms. Every reorder suggestion and cover score is fully explainable with ⓘ Explain modals.
*   **Balanced Network**: Shift regional inventory surpluses to cover shortages in under 2 clicks, avoiding transport delays from the central warehouse.
