## 1. The 5 Levels of Inventory Visibility Maturity

The SMRITI PSV Maturity Assessment helps brand owners and distributors evaluate their operational controls and inventory accuracy.

```
Level 5: Intelligent  ← Auto-balancing transfers & supplier coordination
     ↑
Level 4: Predictive   ← Weeks of Cover & stockout predictions
     ↑
Level 3: Controlled   ← Periodic physical snapshot audits
     ↑
Level 2: Reactive     ← Manual file uploads of secondary sales
     ↑
Level 1: Blind        ← Dispatches only (ERP invoicing only)
```

---

## 2. Maturity Level Definitions & Indicators

### Level 1 — Blind (अंधा चैनल)
*   **Description**: The brand only tracks dispatches from the central factory. Distributor-held stock is a black box.
*   **Key Indicator**: Excess stock accumulates at distributor depots while fast-moving sizes run out of stock.
*   **Technology**: Standard ERP invoicing without secondary visibility.

### Level 2 — Reactive (प्रतिक्रियाशील)
*   **Description**: Distributors manually upload sales spreadsheets weekly or monthly.
*   **Key Indicator**: Reports are always lagging (historical, not real-time).
*   **Technology**: Excel/CSV uploads via email or basic cloud drives.

### Level 3 — Controlled (नियंत्रित)
*   **Description**: Daily or near real-time secondary sales uploads are matched with periodic physical snapshots (every 14 days) in SMRITI.
*   **Key Indicator**: Variance exceptions are caught early and resolved.
*   **Technology**: SMRITI Inventory Visibility Layer active with physical audit workflows.

### Level 4 — Predictive (भविष्य कहनेवाला)
*   **Description**: SMRITI automatically computes daily sales velocities and predicts WOC (Weeks of Cover) by depot and variant.
*   **Key Indicator**: Planners receive stockout warnings 3–7 weeks in advance.
*   **Technology**: SMRITI PSV dashboard, WOC Alerts, and Lead Time simulation active.

### Level 5 — Intelligent (बुद्धिमान नेटवर्क)
*   **Description**: The network automatically generates inter-store transfer recommendations and triggers optimized replenishment orders.
*   **Key Indicator**: Working capital locked in dead stock is kept below 15% across all regional distributors.
*   **Technology**: Network Stock Transfers (NST), dynamic lead time tracking, and automated reorder buffer logic.

## 3. Discovery self-Assessment Questions

Ask your team or prospective brand customer these 5 diagnostic questions:

1.  **Daily Visibility**: Do you know exactly how many units of style-A size 8 are sitting on distributor shelves *today*?
2.  **Weekly Velocity**: Do you know the weekly sell-through rate of your merchandise by region?
3.  **WOC Monitoring**: Can you identify which distributor depots will run out of stock in the next 14 days?
4.  **Capital tracking**: Do you know the exact monetary value of dead stock (aging over 90 days) locked at partner depots?
5.  **Inter-Store Balancing**: If Mumbai has excess stock and Pune has a stockout, can you coordinate a regional transfer in 2 clicks?
