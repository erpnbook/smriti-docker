## 1. Executive Summary
SMRITI Party Stock Visibility (PSV) is a premium, cloud-native **Inventory Visibility Layer** designed to connect brands, distributors, and franchise networks into a single, real-time supply chain system. By decoupling distributor stock tracking from corporate financial books, SMRITI protects margins, reduces stockouts by 60%, and increases distributor ROI from 20% to 26% without increasing brand capital exposure.

---

## 2. Core Business Problems Addressed

1.  **The Distribution Visibility Gap**: Loss of stock visibility once inventory leaves primary warehouses, leading to stockouts of bestsellers and excess stagnation of slow-moving items.
2.  **Working Capital Stagnation**: Stagnant, non-moving variant inventory locks up cash at distributor locations, stalling supply chain flow.
3.  **Fulfillment guessworks**: replenishment is triggered reactively without accurate daily sales velocity data.

---

## 3. SMRITI PSV Key Indicators & Workflows

### A. Weeks of Cover (WOC)
*   **Definition**: Measures how many weeks current channel inventory will last based on average daily sales velocities.
*   *Green Zone (7 - 14 weeks)*: Optimized.
*   *Watch Zone (3 - 7 weeks)*: Depletion warning.
*   *Action Zone (< 3 weeks)*: Critical stockout risk.

### B. Network Stock Transfer (NST)
*   **Definition**: Rebalances inventory across distributor locations by transferring surpluses from overstocked depots to short-supply hubs, protecting margins from localized stockouts.

### C. Reorder & Replenishment Intelligence
*   **Definition**: Automatically triggers replenishment recommendations based on lead times, daily sales velocity, and safety stock buffers.
