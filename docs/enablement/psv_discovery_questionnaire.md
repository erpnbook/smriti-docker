## 1. Meeting Agenda & Overview
This discovery script guides sales teams during initial consultations with Brand Directors, CFOs, and Distribution Heads. The objective is to identify visibility blind spots, variant mismatches, and capital inefficiencies in their current supply chain.

---

## 2. Phase 1: Context Gathering (Company Profile)
1.  How many regional distributors, franchise outlets, or multi-brand outlets (MBOs) are active in your network?
2.  What is your primary product category (e.g. Footwear, Apparel, FMCG)? Do you track products using variant size-color curves?
3.  Which ERP or accounting system serves as your current system of record (e.g. ERPNext, Tally, SAP)?

---

## 3. Phase 2: Uncovering Pain Points (The Visibility Gap)
4.  **The Invoicing Blind Spot**: Once stock leaves your central warehouse and is invoiced, how do you track what has actually been sold to end-consumers?
5.  **The Stockout Penalty**: How do you know when a distributor runs out of standard sizes (e.g. Size 7 and 8) while they still have extreme sizes in stock?
6.  **Guesswork Reorders**: How do distributors decide when to place their next order? Is it based on daily sales velocity metrics or manual guesses?
7.  **Dead Stock Accrual**: What is the current value of stagnant stock sitting unsold in distributor warehouses for over 90 days? Who bears the cost of this dead stock?

---

## 4. Phase 3: Technical & Operational Feasibility
8.  Do your distributors use a POS system? Are they willing to upload daily checkout files in exchange for automated, optimal reorder recommendations?
9.  How often are physical inventory snapshots taken at your distributor locations to reconcile data discrepancies?
10. If Mumbai has a surplus of sneakers and Pune has a stockout, what is your current operational process for coordinating an inter-store transfer?

---

## 5. Phase 4: Business Value Alignment
11. If SMRITI PSV could reduce your bestseller stockouts by 60% and free up 15% of your locked working capital, what would be the financial impact on your annual sales margin?
12. Would a structured, Level 1 **SMRITI Certified Planner Program** help align your distributor staff on weeks of cover and stock management compliance?
